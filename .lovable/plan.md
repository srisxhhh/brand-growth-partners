# Consolidate services and elevate site motion

## Changes
- Replace the separate Websites and Mobile Apps offerings with one **TECH** service covering web, mobile, hosting, launches, maintenance, and updates.
- Merge Video Editing and Graphic Design into **DESIGN & EDITING**, with one combined page and unified process.
- Rework **Personalised UGC** into five stages: taking the brief, finding creators, confirming the script, editing raw files, and final approval.
- Rename founder branding to **Personal & Commercial Branding** and center its message on scroll-stopping concepts and knowing what should be created.
- Keep **Meta Ads** as its own service and renumber the full service set.
- Replace every visible company email with `contact@wellhandled.in`.
- Remove all post-click/dodge status text beneath the founder-page Agree and Naah buttons, leaving only the buttons.
- Add more cinematic, editorial motion inspired by Temple’s pacing: masked text entrances, smoother image reveals, stronger scroll choreography, refined card movement, and restrained transitions.

## Technical details
- Preserve existing lead capture, consent, attribution, analytics, SEO, FAQ, and WhatsApp behavior.
- Redirect legacy service URLs to the matching consolidated pages so old links remain useful.
- Reuse the strongest existing service imagery across the consolidated pages rather than creating unrelated assets.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and verify the result on phone and desktop sizes.
