# Final service and brief updates

## What will change
- Standardize all service names in title case: Tech, Personalised UGC, Personal & Commercial Branding, Design & Editing, Meta Ads, and AI Automation.
- Add AI Automation as a sixth service on the homepage and as a complete service page with deliverables, connected-workflow steps, outcome, FAQs, SEO, imagery, and enquiry actions.
- Replace the one-size-fits-all enquiry form with a service-specific brief while preserving spam protection, campaign attribution, WhatsApp handoff, analytics, thank-you state, and dashboard storage.

## Brief fields
- **Tech:** name, phone or email, build-type dropdown (website, mobile app, landing page, e-commerce store, web app, or other), and an open “Explain your idea” field.
- **Personalised UGC:** name, phone or email, brand name, role/designation, video reference links, and brand website.
- **Personal & Commercial Branding:** the same fields as Personalised UGC.
- **Design & Editing:** name, phone or email, reference page/video link, brand or channel name, and a short-form/long-form selector.
- **Meta Ads:** name, phone or email, average monthly ad-spend budget, and target cities or locations.
- **AI Automation:** name, phone or email, and an open workflow-automation brief.

## Validation and lead handling
- Validate every field before saving or opening WhatsApp, with sensible length and URL checks.
- Store the contact value in the existing email/contact column and compile the remaining answers into readable notes, so the current dashboard continues to show the complete brief without a database restructure.
- Format each WhatsApp message by service so the full brief arrives clearly in chat.

## Quality checks
- Confirm every service card and page uses consistent typography and capitalization.
- Test all six service pages, field validation, dropdowns, submission behavior, dashboard-compatible saved data, legacy service redirects, and phone/laptop layouts.
- Check the final preview for build, runtime, console, and overflow errors.
